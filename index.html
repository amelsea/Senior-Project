About the event

WinterHaus is a festive holiday market featuring local makers, seasonal food, handmade gifts, and activities for the community.

Visitors can explore independent vendors, enjoy seasonal treats, and experience the atmosphere of the holiday season in one welcoming market.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the market free to attend?

Yes. General admission to WinterHaus is free.

Are pets allowed?

Service animals are welcome. Check event guidance for other pets before visiting.

Can I purchase gifts from vendors?

Yes. Vendors offer a variety of handmade goods, seasonal foods, decorations, and gifts.
